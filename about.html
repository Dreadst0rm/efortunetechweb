About efortune.tech

Technical depth. Strategic clarity. Measurable outcomes.

Our Approach

efortune.tech was founded on a simple observation: most technology challenges are architectural, not purely implementation problems. Teams often know how to build software but struggle with the decisions that determine long-term success.

We work as senior technical partners. Our consultants have led architecture initiatives at scale, built platform teams, and guided organizations through complex migrations and transformations. We prioritize clarity, pragmatism, and knowledge transfer over dependency.

Based in Chicago, we serve clients across North America in fintech, healthcare, retail, and enterprise software.

Core Principles

Architecture First

We focus on the decisions that are expensive to reverse. Good architecture creates optionality; poor architecture creates constraints.

Pragmatic Over Perfect

Ideal architectures that never ship have no value. We design for the constraints of real organizations and delivery timelines.

Knowledge Transfer

Our goal is to leave your team stronger. Engagements include documentation, decision records, and working sessions designed for lasting capability.

Outcome Oriented

We measure success by business and engineering outcomes: reduced cost, faster delivery, improved reliability, and clearer technical direction.
