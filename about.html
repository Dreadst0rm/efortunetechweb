About Ed Fortune

Turning technology strategy into measurable business outcomes.

What I Do for Customers

I help organizations convert technology strategy into disciplined, executable results. Over 20 years I have developed and delivered technology strategy across enterprise environments—guiding architecture decisions, modernizing infrastructure, and aligning technical choices with business value.

Clients engage me when they need clear direction on cloud and infrastructure modernization, identity and access architecture (including Entra ID and passwordless approaches), resilient data center and network designs, legacy system transitions, and pragmatic guidance on secure AI adoption. The focus is always on outcomes: lower cost, higher reliability, reduced risk, and faster progress—without creating dependency.

Based in the Chicago area, I work with leadership and engineering teams to make architecture decisions that respect organizational history while building for the future.

How Customers Benefit

Strategy to Execution

Translate high-level technology goals into concrete architecture roadmaps and decision frameworks that teams can actually deliver against.

Resilient Infrastructure

Design and modernize cloud, identity, network, and data center environments for reliability, security, and cost efficiency.

Pragmatic Modernization

Guide legacy-to-modern transitions (including SQL, Windows, and platform shifts) that balance risk, timeline, and long-term optionality.

Unbiased Advisory

Provide experienced perspective on identity, AI incorporation, vendor choices, and enterprise tech stack decisions—grounded in what has worked in real organizations.
