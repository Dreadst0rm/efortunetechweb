Case Studies

Selected engagements demonstrating architecture and strategy impact.

CLOUD ARCHITECTURE · FINTECH

Multi-Cloud Consolidation & FinOps Implementation

Challenge: A growing payments company operated workloads across three cloud providers with inconsistent standards, rising costs, and unclear ownership.

Approach: We conducted a full cloud portfolio assessment, defined a target architecture centered on a primary provider with selective multi-cloud for specific capabilities, and implemented tagging, budgeting, and cost allocation practices.

Results:

  • 40% reduction in monthly cloud spend within 9 months
  • Improved availability from 99.5% to 99.95%
  • Established clear ownership model and FinOps operating cadence
  • Reduced time to provision new environments from weeks to hours
SOFTWARE ARCHITECTURE · ENTERPRISE

Incremental Monolith Decomposition

Challenge: A 12-year-old product had become a tightly coupled monolith that slowed feature delivery and made independent team scaling difficult.

Approach: Rather than a full rewrite, we identified natural bounded contexts, established an API gateway and event backbone, and guided teams through a strangler-fig extraction pattern over 18 months.

Results:

  • Release cycle reduced from bi-weekly to daily for extracted services
  • Team autonomy increased; three independent product squads now deploy without coordination
  • Defect rates in new modules dropped significantly due to clearer boundaries
  • Preserved business continuity throughout the transition
AI STRATEGY · HEALTHCARE

From Experiments to Production ML Platform

Challenge: Data science teams had built multiple promising models that never reached production due to lack of infrastructure, governance, and integration patterns.

Approach: We defined an MLOps reference architecture, prioritized two high-value use cases, established model governance processes, and guided the first production deployment with monitoring and retraining pipelines.

Results:

  • First production model delivered measurable operational savings within 90 days of engagement start
  • Reusable ML platform reduced time-to-production for subsequent models by ~60%
  • Clear governance framework satisfied compliance requirements
