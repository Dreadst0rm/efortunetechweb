Technical Consulting Services

Strategic guidance and hands-on architecture expertise across the technology landscape.

Cloud Architecture & Migration

We design cloud platforms that balance cost, reliability, security, and developer velocity. Our engagements typically cover:

  • Multi-cloud and hybrid architecture design
  • Workload migration strategies (lift-and-shift, re-platform, re-architect)
  • FinOps and cost optimization frameworks
  • Landing zone design and governance
  • Disaster recovery and business continuity planning

Clients typically see 25–45% infrastructure cost reduction within the first year while improving availability metrics.

Software Architecture

Sustainable software systems require deliberate architectural decisions. We help teams:

  • Define bounded contexts and service boundaries
  • Choose appropriate architectural styles (modular monolith, microservices, event-driven)
  • Establish technical standards and decision frameworks
  • Conduct architecture reviews and risk assessments
  • Design for observability, resilience, and evolvability

Our approach prioritizes incremental improvement over big-bang rewrites.

AI & Machine Learning Strategy

Moving from AI experiments to production value requires disciplined strategy. We support:

  • Use case identification and prioritization
  • Data readiness and pipeline architecture
  • MLOps platform design
  • Model governance and responsible AI practices
  • Build vs. buy analysis for AI capabilities
